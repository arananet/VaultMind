# Security & Community Organization Guide — VaultMind

> **NOTE**: The greatest survival asset is community. Lone individuals
> have historically poor survival rates in prolonged crises. Organize,
> cooperate, and build trust.

## Community Organization

### Immediate Post-Event (First 72 Hours)
1. Account for all community members
2. Identify injuries — triage and treat
3. Secure water and food supplies
4. Establish communication (runners, radio, Meshtastic)
5. Assign rotating watch/security
6. Identify key skills in the group (medical, mechanical, agricultural)

### Governance Structure (Small Community, 20-100 people)
- **Council**: 5-7 elected/appointed members; rotate quarterly
- **Working groups**: Security, Medical, Food/Water, Construction, Comms
- **Decisions**: Majority vote on council; community vote on major issues
- **Conflict resolution**: Designated mediator (not a council member)
- **Rules**: Written, posted publicly, enforceable, fair

### Resource Management
- **Inventory**: Catalog all community resources immediately
- **Rationing**: Establish fair distribution before scarcity hits
- **Trade**: Standardize barter values early (labor hours as currency)
- **Contribution**: Everyone contributes according to ability
- **Reserves**: Always maintain 30-day emergency food/water reserve

## Perimeter Security

### Layered Defense Model
```
Zone 4: Observation (500m+)     — Early warning, scouts
Zone 3: Deterrence (100-500m)   — Obstacles, noise makers, cleared fields of view
Zone 2: Detection (10-100m)     — Trip wires, cameras, dogs, sensors
Zone 1: Hardened (0-10m)        — Fortified positions, barriers, access control
```

### Early Warning Systems (No Electricity Required)
- **Trip wire + cans**: Fishing line at ankle height, tin cans with rocks
- **Gravel paths**: Surround perimeter — impossible to walk silently on gravel
- **Geese**: Seriously — geese are aggressive and loud; better than dogs for alerts
- **Mirror/glass shards**: Placed to reflect light when disturbed
- **Observation posts**: Elevated positions with clear sight lines

### Access Control
- Single entry/exit point for visitors
- Challenge and verification procedure
- Escort policy for unknown visitors
- After-dark protocols (no entry without pre-arrangement)

## Watch Protocols

### Standard Watch Rotation
| Watch   | Time        | Duration | Notes                           |
|---------|-------------|----------|---------------------------------|
| First   | 20:00-00:00 | 4 hours  | Transition from day to night    |
| Second  | 00:00-04:00 | 4 hours  | Deepest night; double if able   |
| Third   | 04:00-08:00 | 4 hours  | Dawn; most common attack time   |

### Watch Rules
1. Minimum 2 people per watch (prevents sleep, provides backup)
2. No one pulls back-to-back watches
3. Watch positions must have cover/concealment
4. Defined communication plan (radio, whistle codes, runner)
5. Challenge/password system changes daily
6. Written log of all observations

### Alert Levels
- **Green**: Normal operations, standard watch
- **Yellow**: Credible threat detected; increase watch, restrict movement
- **Red**: Active threat; all hands to defensive positions

## Conflict De-escalation

### Principles
1. **Stay calm**: Your tone sets the other person's tone
2. **Create space**: Physical distance reduces tension
3. **Listen first**: Let them speak; acknowledge their concerns
4. **Find common ground**: "We both want safety for our families"
5. **Offer options**: Give choices, not ultimatums
6. **Time**: Most situations improve with time; delay if possible

### When Approached by Unknown Groups
1. Meet at a distance from your base (neutral ground)
2. Show strength through organization, not aggression
3. Have overwatch (visible or concealed backup)
4. Be willing to trade; it builds trust
5. Set clear boundaries about your community space
6. Never reveal your full resources or capabilities

### Barter and Trade
- **High-value barter items** to stockpile:
  - Salt, sugar, coffee, alcohol, tobacco
  - Antibiotics, pain medication, first aid supplies
  - Seeds, tools, ammunition
  - Knowledge/skills (the ultimate non-depletable resource)
- **Trade rules**:
  - Trade from a position of surplus, never desperation
  - Meet on neutral ground
  - Never trade alone
  - Fair dealing builds long-term alliances

## Information Security (OPSEC)

### What to Protect
- Location and size of your community
- Food and water reserves (quantity and location)
- Medical supplies inventory
- Defensive capabilities and positions
- Communication schedules and frequencies

### Practices
- Need-to-know basis for sensitive information
- Vary patrol routes and times
- Minimize visible signs of prosperity (smoke, light, noise)
- Bury or conceal supply caches (multiple locations)
- Encrypt radio communications (Meshtastic supports AES-256)
- Sanitize trash — it reveals what you have

## Mental Health in Crisis

### Common Reactions (Normal, Not Weakness)
- Sleep disturbance, nightmares
- Hypervigilance, startle response
- Grief, anger, emotional numbness
- Difficulty concentrating
- Physical symptoms: headaches, stomach issues

### Community Support
- Regular group meetings (normalcy, connection)
- Rotate demanding duties (prevent burnout)
- Maintain routines (meals, work shifts, rest)
- Celebrate small wins (successful harvest, repairs completed)
- Children need extra structure and reassurance
- Physical activity helps process stress
- Journaling: writing reduces psychological burden
