# Construction & Shelter Guide — VaultMind

> **WARNING**: Structural failure kills. When in doubt, overbuild.
> Never occupy a structure you are not confident in.

## Emergency Shelter Types

### Debris Hut (Immediate, 1-2 hours)
1. Find a ridgepole: straight branch, 3m long, sturdy
2. Prop one end on a stump/rock at waist height; other end on ground
3. Lean branches along both sides at 45° angle (ribs)
4. Layer debris (leaves, grass, pine needles) 1m thick on ribs
5. Add more branches on top to hold debris in place
6. Fill interior with dry leaves/grass (insulation from ground)
7. Block entrance with a debris plug

### A-Frame Tarp Shelter (Quick, 15 minutes)
1. Tie a ridgeline between two trees at chest height
2. Drape tarp over ridgeline
3. Stake corners at 45° angle from ridgeline
4. Angle opening away from prevailing wind

### Long-Term Structures

**Wattle and Daub (oldest permanent building technique)**
1. Set vertical posts (10-15cm diameter) at 30cm intervals
2. Weave horizontal sticks between posts (wattle)
3. Mix daub: 1 part clay, 1 part sand, straw/grass for binding
4. Apply daub to both sides of wattle, 5-10cm thick
5. Let dry slowly (2-4 weeks) — too fast and it cracks
6. Seal with lime wash (burned limestone + water)

## Concrete and Mortar (Without a Hardware Store)

### Portland Cement Substitute — Lime Mortar
1. **Burn limestone**: Heat limestone rocks to 900°C+ in a kiln (4-8 hours)
   - This produces quickite (calcium oxide / CaO)
2. **Slake the lime**: Add water carefully to quicklime
   - **Risk**: Extremely exothermic — can boil water and cause severe burns
   - Produces slaked lime (calcium hydroxide)
3. **Make mortar**: Mix 1 part slaked lime : 3 parts sand
4. **Apply**: Use between bricks, stones, or blocks
5. **Cure**: Lime mortar hardens by absorbing CO2 from air (slow: weeks to months)
6. Not as strong as Portland cement but has been used for thousands of years

### Roman Concrete (Pozzolanic Cement)
- Mix volcanic ash (or crusite pottery dust) with slaked lime
- Produces hydraulic cement that sets underwater
- Stronger and more durable than lime mortar alone
- Ratio: 2 parts pozzolan : 1 part lime : 3 parts aggregate

### Rammed Earth Walls
1. Build wooden forms (like concrete forms) 60cm wide
2. Fill with slightly damp earth (mix: 30% clay, 70% sand/gravel)
3. Tamp/ram in 10cm layers using a heavy pole
4. Remove forms after 24 hours
5. Walls are load-bearing at 30cm+ thickness
6. Protect from rain with wide roof overhangs or lime plaster

## Basic Plumbing

### Gravity-Fed Water System
1. Place water tank at highest practical point
2. Run pipe downhill to point of use
3. **Head pressure**: 1 meter of height = 0.1 bar (~1.5 PSI)
4. 10 meters head = decent shower pressure
5. Use PVC pipe or bamboo (sealed joints with tar/resin)

### Composting Toilet
1. Build a box seat over a 5-gallon bucket
2. After each use, cover with sawdust, wood shavings, or dry leaves
3. When bucket is full, empty into compost pile
4. Compost for 12+ months before using on gardens (pathogen die-off)
5. **Risk**: Never use fresh humanure on food crops — disease transmission

### Septic System (Basic)
1. **Septic tank**: Sealed concrete or plastic tank (1000L minimum)
2. Inlet pipe from building; outlet pipe to drain field
3. **Drain field**: Perforated pipe in gravel-filled trenches
4. Minimum 30m from any water source
5. Tank needs pumping every 3-5 years

## Electrical Wiring Basics

### Wire Sizing (12V DC Systems)
| Load (Amps) | Max Distance | Wire Size (AWG) |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| 5A          | 5m          | 14 AWG          |
| 10A         | 5m          | 10 AWG          |
| 20A         | 5m          | 8 AWG           |
| 5A          | 15m         | 10 AWG          |
| 10A         | 15m         | 6 AWG           |

### Safety Rules
- **Always fuse every positive wire** at the power source
- Use fuses rated for 125% of expected load
- Never run wiring through standing water
- Separate AC and DC circuits completely
- **Risk**: 12V DC can still start fires through short circuits

### Simple 12V Solar Wiring
```
Solar Panel → Charge Controller → Battery Bank
                                       ↓
                              12V Fuse Box → Loads (lights, USB, radio)
                                       ↓
                              Inverter → 120V/240V AC (if needed)
```

## Sanitation Systems

### Latrine Construction
1. Dig pit: 1m x 1m x 2m deep minimum
2. Minimum 30m from water sources, downhill from camp
3. Build seat/platform over pit
4. After each use, add 2-3cm of soil, ash, or lime
5. When pit is within 30cm of surface, fill and cap with soil
6. Mark location — do not dig near it for 2+ years

### Waste Water (Grey Water)
- Kitchen and washing water can be filtered through:
  1. Grease trap (bucket with baffles)
  2. Sand/gravel filter bed
  3. Planted reed bed (if space available)
- Output is suitable for irrigation (not drinking)

### Disease Prevention
- Wash hands after latrine use (soap + water or ash + water)
- Separate cooking water from all other water uses
- Keep food storage areas clean and sealed from insects/rodents
- Isolate sick individuals — separate latrine if diarrheal illness
